[Street Piano]

Hotaru: (I need to get better... Better still.)
Hotaru: (To make everyone's wishes come true, I need to get better... Better—)
Audience A: Wow... Who is she? Is she a professional?
Audience B: I've never heard piano like that before...
Hotaru: Thank you so much! Oh...?
Mahoro: Hey! I came to watch.
Hotaru: Mahoro-san! Hi!
Mahoro: Good work, Hotaru-chan. Even on days without band practice, you come here and play piano?
Hotaru: Yes. I can practice at home, of course. But only here can I play for someone and let the music reach them.
Mahoro: That is so admirable... Playing all by yourself in front of people you don't even know.
Mahoro: I'd probably get too scared and start shaking.
Hotaru: Hehe. I was the same at first.
Hotaru: Unlike a competition, you do not know who might listen... or whether anyone will be there at all.
Hotaru: I never would have thought to try it either... until Kanata-san invited me.
Mahoro: I see... Kanata played here too, didn't she?
Hotaru: Yes... She made so many people smile.
Mahoro: Kanata... she really never told anyone what she was doing...
Mahoro: We had never even heard that she played at a street piano.
Mahoro: Then again, we were pretty used to Kanata suddenly starting something new.
Mahoro: She was really something when we were little, you know? "I'll find a tsuchinoko!" or "I'll become a writer and win some big award!"
Hotaru: Hehe... Is that so?
Mahoro: Yeah. And, well... Kanata must have had friends besides us too...
Mahoro: I just thought it might be wrong to intrude, so I never ended up going to watch.
Hotaru: ...
Mahoro: I wanted to hear it. Kanata and you, Hotaru-chan... playing piano together.
Mahoro: If I had gone to watch back then... maybe we would have met much sooner.
Hotaru: If only... Back then...
Hotaru: (If I had met Mahoro-san... if I had met everyone in millsage...)
Hotaru: (Or...)
Hotaru: (If I had never met Kanata-san...)
Hotaru: (Then...)
Mahoro: Hotaru-chan?
Hotaru: Oh! Sorry.
Hotaru: (No. There is no point thinking about "if only.")
Hotaru: Mahoro-san, do you have a request? I'll play anything you would like to hear!
Mahoro: Huh... Really? Then... Hmm... Oh, I know!
Hotaru: (I am going to give this story a happy ending!)
