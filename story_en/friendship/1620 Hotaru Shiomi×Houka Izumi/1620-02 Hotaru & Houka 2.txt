Houka: Those are Mahoro-san's. She often looks through the photos of delicious food at all the sightseeing spots.

Houka: Ah, Nagi-san brought those in.
Hotaru: We can watch movies here too?
Houka: Yes. We have not used it recently, but I believe there is a player beneath the television.
Houka: We used to watch them together quite often.
Hotaru: I see... So that is how it was.
Houka: Do you enjoy movies, Hotaru-san?
Hotaru: I have not really watched very many...
Houka: Oh my. Are they simply not to your taste?
Hotaru: No, it is not that. I just hardly ever go to the movie theater.
Hotaru: Sometimes my family watches whatever is on television on weekend nights...
Houka: I see. What kinds of films do you usually watch?
Hotaru: Um... I remember watching family-friendly animated movies and fantasy films from overseas.
Houka: How lovely. Those are classic genres with so many wonderful films.
Hotaru: Do you like movies, Houka-san?
Houka: I do, though I only go to the theater from time to time.
Hotaru: What kinds of movies do you watch?
Houka: Zombie movies♪
Hotaru: Z-Zombies...!?
Hotaru: Zombie movies are horror movies, right...? So you like scary films?
Houka: No, not exactly. I like zombie movies, but I am not especially fond of zombies themselves.
Hotaru: Then, um...?
Houka: People who are attacked by zombies. I find the moments when their true nature comes to light rather fascinating... That's it♪
Hotaru: I-I see...?
Houka: Everyone carries desires within them. But in everyday life, we bind ourselves with one reason or another...
Houka: Then, when people like that are placed between life and death by a zombie attack, their true selves emerge.
Houka: Some flee before anyone else, some turn on their companions, and some mercilessly wipe out the zombies...
Houka: They all look so very happy♪
Hotaru: Um... I suppose that is one way to enjoy them...?
Houka: Hehe. If you would like, I can bring you one of my recommendations sometime.
Hotaru: A-A recommendation...?
Houka: Being able to share something you love with another person. I am sure that is a very happy thing indeed♪
Hotaru: Oh...! I understand.
Hotaru: I want to know the things you love too, Houka-san!
Houka: My. This promises to be quite delightful♪
