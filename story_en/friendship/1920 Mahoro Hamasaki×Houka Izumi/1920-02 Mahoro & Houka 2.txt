(Background change)

[Road Along the Shore]

Mahoro: Oh, Houka-chan?
Houka: Oh my, Mahoro-san. What a coincidence. Are you out for a walk?
Mahoro: Yeah. The weather is so nice, I thought I would get out for a change of pace. What about you, Houka-chan?
Houka: I heard there is a well-regarded burger place nearby. I thought I might pay it a visit.
Mahoro: Oh, I did not know that. It is not a chain, right?
Houka: No. I hear they are very particular about every detail.
Houka: They use only fresh vegetables from contracted farms. And their patties are 100% beef, with no fillers at all.
Houka: Their buns are baked to order, so I hear the wait is more than twenty minutes.
Mahoro: Y-You really know your stuff...!
Houka: Hehe. I merely read what was written on their website.
Mahoro: I see... Do places like that usually need reservations too?
Houka: That is the thing... Apparently, they do not take reservations at all.
Mahoro: What? So you have to line up no matter what!?
Houka: Yes. But that only means it is good enough to make people feel it is worth the wait... It certainly raises one's expectations.
Mahoro: O-Oh...
Houka: Does that not appeal to you very much, Mahoro-san?
Mahoro: Hmm... I get that it sounds impressive, but I do not think it has really clicked for me...
Mahoro: I have only had burgers from chains, but I still think they are plenty delicious.
Houka: Oh, what a shame. It is good to know the real thing, you know?
Mahoro: The real thing...?
Houka: Yes. Affordable chain restaurants have their merits as well, but if that is all one knows, one's senses may grow dull.
Houka: Experiencing something of the highest quality lets one realize anew that one is ordinary, and that there are greater heights in the world.
Mahoro: Um... So you are not just going because you want to eat something delicious...?
Houka: Hehe. Of course I am interested in delicious food.
Houka: But it is not only that. What matters to me is the shape of the world that food reveals, and understanding where I stand within it♪
Mahoro: That is... a pretty complicated way of putting it...
Houka: That said, this is only my own way of thinking. I think chain restaurants are lovely too.
Mahoro: The shape of the world... Where I stand...
Houka: Well then, if you will excuse me.
Mahoro: Ah, wait! Houka-chan!
Mahoro: Um... Could I come with you to that place?
Houka: Oh my... I thought it had not quite caught your interest?
Mahoro: Ahaha, that is true. But after hearing you talk about it, Houka-chan, I have started getting curious.
Mahoro: I am getting hungry too... And I kind of want to find out what you mean by "the real thing," Houka-chan.
Houka: My...
Mahoro: Sorry for asking so suddenly. Is that not okay?
Houka: No, not at all~ It is not often that Mahoro-san and I get to have lunch together.
Mahoro: Oh! What a relief~
Houka: Hehe. I have a feeling today is going to be quite delightful♪
