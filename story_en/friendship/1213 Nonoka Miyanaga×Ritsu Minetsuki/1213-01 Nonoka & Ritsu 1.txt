[Before Band Practice]

Nonoka: Ricchan...

Ritsu: Really...?
Nonoka: Yes. Whenever we play guitar together in practice, or when I watch Ricchan's guitar streams...

Ritsu: Thank you.
Nonoka: That's gotta be because you practice properly every single day!
Ritsu: Don't you do the same?

Ritsu: Let's see...
Ritsu: I make sure to do the basics every day, like practicing to a click and running through fingering exercises.
Nonoka: Bravooo...!! Magnificent...!!
Ritsu: You really don't need to applaud...
Nonoka: Oh, it absolutely is! Nono-chan is giving you a standing ovation!
Nonoka: Training steadily, day after day, one little step at a time...
Nonoka: Just like doing bunny hops across the field... Wait!!
Nonoka: Let's call it the Bunny-Hop Technique!
Ritsu: The Bunny-Hop Technique... That's a good name.
Ritsu: I think your guitar playing is wonderful too, Nono-chan.
Ritsu: It's passionate and dynamic. Your strumming is powerful and completely fearless.
Ritsu: It's like watching a pro drain noodles... snapping the noodle basket down in one clean motion... Wait!!
Ritsu: This is... the Extra-Firm Downstroke...!!
Nonoka: Extra... firm...!!
Nonoka: So that's it... Nono-chan's guitar was extra firm all along...
Ritsu: ...!
Nonoka: Ricchan, thank you. You helped Nono-chan realize something important.
Ritsu: Not at all.
Ritsu: Today's practice will be a dream collaboration between the Bunny-Hop Technique and the Extra-Firm Downstroke.
Nonoka: Wait!! Then that means...!
Ritsu & Nonoka: The Extra-Firm Bunny-Hop Technique!!
Ritsu: Hehe... Now I'm looking forward to hearing how we sound together.
Nonoka: Yeah! Let's have a total blast~~!
